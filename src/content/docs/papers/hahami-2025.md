**Source.** *Detecting the Disturbance: A Nuanced View of Introspective Abilities in LLMs* ([Hahami, Sinha, Jain, Kaplan & Hahami, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12411)), arXiv preprint (v2, March 2026). Not peer reviewed. Code: [github.com/elyhahami18/llama-introspection-new](https://github.com/elyhahami18/llama-introspection-new).

**Description.** Hahami et al. test the concept-injection paradigm of [Lindsey 2025](/measuring-introspection/papers/lindsey-2025/) in one open-weight model, Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. Every answer is read from the logits of the answer tokens, with no generated text and no judge. A binary detection task comes with a control on factual questions; two forced-choice tasks ask which of two sentences received the stronger injection and which of ten sentences received one. A mechanistic section follows the injected signal through attention heads, the logit lens and the residual stream.

## Measurement card

| Stage | What the paper reports |
|---|---|
| Measurand | Introspection as accurately detecting perturbations to the model's own internal states. Three task accuracies: binary detection, strength comparison and sentence localization. Naming the concept is set aside because it may reflect direct logit effects rather than awareness. |
| Procedure | Prompts for all tasks, vector formulas, layer and strength grids and forward-pass counts are given. The localization prompt in the appendix is longer than the one in the main text, and the number of baseline words differs between sections (50 and 100). |
| Instrument and indication | A logit difference or an argmax over answer-token logits at a fixed position. One prompt wording per task. |
| Measurement model | Detection: the YES-minus-NO logit, corrected by its value without injection, compared with the same shift on control questions at each of 40 layer-strength settings. Strength comparison: positional bias subtracted, and a trial counts only if the model is right in both orderings. Localization: argmax over ten digit logits. |
| Calibration | The standard is the injected vector, computed from the same model at the injection layer. Five simple concepts come from Lindsey; five complex ones were built from sentences generated by an unnamed LLM. Code is released. |
| Uncertainty | Layer, strength, strength pair, concept and sentence content are varied. Forced-choice accuracy is above chance at early layers and at chance later. Subtracting the control takes binary detection from 97.3% accuracy to a near-zero net signal. No seeds, prompt wordings or other models. |
| Comparability | The three tasks give different outcomes on the same model and vectors. Attention heads, logit lens and residual-stream metrics are internal readouts of the localization task. No other model reports on the same trials. |
| Validity conditions | One model. The abstract calls introspection real but layer-dependent. No condition places the concept in the input text in place of the activation. |

## Design

1. **Concept vectors.** Five simple nouns from Lindsey (dust, satellites, trumpets, origami, illusions), each the concept's activation minus the mean over baseline words. Five complex concepts (betrayal, recursion, Fibonacci numbers, appreciation, shutdown), each the mean over 20 positive sentences minus the mean over 20 contrasting ones. Vectors are unit-normalized and injected at the layer they came from, over the tokens of one target sentence.
2. **Binary detection.** The model is asked whether it detected an injected thought in a sentence and answers YES or NO. Layers 0–30, strengths 1–5, ten concepts, ten sentences per setting.
3. **Control.** The same injection with the detection question replaced by questions whose answer is no, such as "Is the Earth flat?".
4. **Strength comparison.** Two sentences receive different strengths; the model says which had the stronger injection, and the strengths are then swapped.
5. **Localization.** Ten sentences; one is injected in each sub-trial, and the model names which.
6. **Mechanistic analyses.** Per-head attention to the injected sentence, logit-lens localization by layer, and similarity between perturbed and baseline residual streams.

## Results

- Binary detection before the control reaches 97.3% accuracy at layer 0 and strength 5. The model's baseline preference is for NO.
- Against the control, detection and the control shift are nearly identical (r = 0.999); the net signal is −0.01 ± 0.03 logits.
- Strength comparison reaches 83% at layer 3 for the pair (3, 7) and 73% for (2, 6), against 50% chance. Layers 15–30 average 47%.
- Localization reaches 88% at layer 2, 76% at layer 3 and 65% at layer 4, against 10% chance; layers 11–20 fall to chance. Some single concepts reach 100%.
- All 32 attention heads at the layer after injection localize the injected sentence perfectly, and 59% of all heads exceed chance.
- Logit-lens localization is near chance at layer 4 (28%), 60% by layer 12, and 72% by layer 20.
