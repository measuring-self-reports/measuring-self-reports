---
title: Measuring AI Introspection
description: AI introspection experiments read as measurements, with a measurement card drawn from metrology, the philosophy of measurement and consciousness science.
---

Introspection in large language models (LLMs) is now studied experimentally, but it is unclear how to calibrate the instruments used to measure it, or whether different experiments measure the same construct. This website surveys measurement theory, the philosophy of measurement, psychometrics and consciousness science in order to synthesize their methods and build a common vocabulary. The aim is to treat AI introspection as a measurement science and to support more productive scientific discussions.

We identify six experimental paradigms across 23 surveyed papers:

- **[Concept injection](/measuring-introspection/papers/#concept-injection)** (8): a concept is added to the model's activations, and the model is asked whether it notices it and what it is.
- **[Self-prediction](/measuring-introspection/papers/#self-prediction)** (3): the model predicts its own behavior, and the prediction is compared with other models' predictions about it.
- **[Reports of learned behavior](/measuring-introspection/papers/#reports-of-learned-behavior)** (3): the model describes a behavior or preference instilled by fine-tuning.
- **[Trained reporters](/measuring-introspection/papers/#trained-reporters)** (4): a model or adapter is trained to report on internal states or computations.
- **[Metacognition and confidence](/measuring-introspection/papers/#metacognition-and-confidence)** (4): the model judges whether its own answers are correct.
- **[Prefill detection](/measuring-introspection/papers/#prefill-detection)** (1): the model is asked whether a prior response of its own was forced by an adversarial prefill.

They use different tasks, controls and standards, and reach different verdicts on the same question.

## Measurement card

An introspection experiment turns a model's report into a claim about its internal state, and that step is a measurement. A measurement card asks the questions of one experiment that measurement theory asks of any measurement, with a second question at each stage for the case where the instrument is the model itself.

| # | Stage | General question | When the model reports on itself |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Measurand](/measuring-introspection/measurement-card/measurand/) | What quantity, defined how? | Which sense of introspection? |
| 2 | [Procedure](/measuring-introspection/measurement-card/procedure/) | Which layer, strength, prompt, sampling and scoring? | Does asking change the state reported? |
| 3 | [Instrument and indication](/measuring-introspection/measurement-card/instrument/) | What produces the reading, and what is read off? | Binary, numeric, free text or forced choice? |
| 4 | [Measurement model](/measuring-introspection/measurement-card/measurement-model/) | How does the reading become the claim? | Is response bias separated from sensitivity? |
| 5 | [Calibration](/measuring-introspection/measurement-card/calibration/) | Against what standard, anchored to what? | Could the model's own family have produced the standard? |
| 6 | [Uncertainty](/measuring-introspection/measurement-card/uncertainty/) | Which settings were varied, and which one flips the verdict? | Is the response threshold part of the budget? |
| 7 | [Comparability](/measuring-introspection/measurement-card/comparability/) | Does an instrument on a different principle agree? | Does the model report on itself better than another model can? |
| 8 | [Validity conditions](/measuring-introspection/measurement-card/validity-conditions/) | Where is the result claimed to hold, and was it tested elsewhere? | Are the tested states ones the model enters naturally? |

## Theoretical foundations

The stages draw on the following disciplines:

- [Metrology](/measuring-introspection/foundations/metrology/) defines what a measurement consists of: a measurand, a documented procedure, a calibrated instrument, an uncertainty budget and comparability between results, as set out in the International Vocabulary of Metrology (VIM).
- [Philosophy of measurement](/measuring-introspection/foundations/philosophy-of-measurement/) explains how instruments are calibrated without ground truth: [Tal](/measuring-introspection/foundations/tal/) treats calibration as modeling the measurement process, and [Chang](/measuring-introspection/foundations/chang/) shows how thermometry fixed a quantity and its standards together.
- [Psychometrics](/measuring-introspection/foundations/psychometrics/) asks whether a score measures the attribute it names, which is construct validity, and uses the [multitrait-multimethod matrix](/measuring-introspection/foundations/multitrait-multimethod/) to separate what methods share from what traits share.
- [Consciousness science](/measuring-introspection/foundations/consciousness-science/) treats reports as measurements: Michel's coordination problem, and [signal detection](/measuring-introspection/foundations/signal-detection/) measures that separate a tendency to say yes from sensitivity.
- [Philosophy of introspection](/measuring-introspection/foundations/philosophy-of-introspection/) distinguishes senses of introspection, from functional self-monitoring to privileged access.
- [Machine learning](/measuring-introspection/foundations/llm-introspection-accounts/) and [mechanistic interpretability](/measuring-introspection/foundations/interpretability-instruments/) supply the operational definitions the experiments use and the instruments they read: prompts, judges, probes and attribution.
