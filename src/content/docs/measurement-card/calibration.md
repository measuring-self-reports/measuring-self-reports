VIM is the [International Vocabulary of Metrology](https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/2071204/JCGM_200_2012.pdf). LLM is large language model.

## What the stage asks

1. **What supplies the reference values?** Calibration relates the values of a standard to an instrument's indications, then uses that relation to get results from indications (VIM 2.39). In introspection experiments the standard is one of: a value the experimenter constructs (random preference weights, a planted trigger), a vector the experimenter injects, a label from human raters, a label from an LLM judge, or an interventional readout of the model's own computation.
2. **What kind of calibration?** [Tal (2017)](https://philpapers.org/rec/TALCMT) distinguishes three. **Black-box**: the instrument is treated as an input-output unit, and the reports are correlated with the standard. **White-box**: the paper models how the report is produced, module by module. **Coherent**: the instrument is placed in a larger network of comparisons among instruments, some of which may be standards.
3. **Could the model's own family have produced the standard?** A concept vector extracted from the model under test, a judge from the same model family, and training labels generated by the model itself are all standards the measured system helped make. The code records which.
4. **Is calibration kept apart from adjustment?** The VIM warns against confusing calibration with adjusting the instrument, often mistakenly called "self-calibration" (VIM 2.39, Note 2). Fine-tuning a model to report injections, with the injection label as the training target, adjusts the instrument. Accuracy after that training is agreement with the target it was trained on, and the stage records that the standard and the training target are the same.
5. **Traceability.** A traceable result can be related to a reference through an unbroken, documented chain of calibrations (VIM 2.41). For an introspection result the chain runs from the reported number through the scoring rule, the judge and its prompt, the per-trial outputs and the model version, and, for a trained-in behavior, a check that the model acquired the behavior it is said to report. A missing link breaks the chain.

### Codes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| constructed | The experimenter sets the true value (random weights, a planted trigger, a sampling temperature). |
| injected | The standard is an activation-space intervention whose presence and content the experimenter controls. |
| human-labeled | Human raters supply the reference. |
| LLM-judged | A language model supplies the reference or scores agreement with it. |
| interventional | The reference is the model's own behavior or computation under a controlled intervention (patching, ablation, counterfactual input). |
| independent | Neither the measured model nor its family produced the standard. |
| shared family | The standard or its extraction comes from the same model family as the measured model. |
| shared judge | The same judge model scores both the standard and the report. |
| self-generated | The standard is produced by the measured model itself. |
| traceability documented / partial / none | Code, per-trial outputs, judge prompts and model versions are all released; some are; none are. |

## Examples from the papers

- **Injected standard, judge from the measured family.** [Lindsey 2025](/measuring-self-reports/papers/lindsey-2025/) uses concept vectors extracted from the model under test as the standard and grades responses with Claude Sonnet 4. The measured models are Claude models.
- **Two standards side by side.** [Plunkett et al. 2025](/measuring-self-reports/papers/plunkett-2025/) have a constructed standard, random target weights fixed at the start, and a behavioral one, the weights estimated from the fine-tuned model's choices. Against the behavioral standard, GPT-4o's reports correlate at r = .54.
- **A white-box standard.** [Song, Hu & Mahowald 2025](/measuring-self-reports/papers/song-hu-mahowald-2025/) score a prompted judgment against the model's own string probabilities, beyond what a model with nearly identical knowledge would predict.
- **An upper-bound standard.** [Ji-An et al. 2025](/measuring-self-reports/papers/ji-an-2025/) compare the model's reports with an ideal observer that reads the activations directly, and find the reports significantly worse.
- **A validated LLM judge.** [Kutsyk & Zieliński 2026](/measuring-self-reports/papers/kutsyk-2026/) check their judge against human raters (κ = 0.53) and report the direction of its error: it gives about 25% more positive labels than the human majority.
- **Traceability to the current state.** [Guo et al. 2026](/measuring-self-reports/papers/guo-2026/) ask whether a trained self-explanation follows the model's current behavior or the labels it was trained on. Where the two disagree, the regularized model sides with its own behavior 79%, 82% and 63% of the time across three settings.

## What the stage does not establish

Agreement with a standard shows that the report tracks the standard. When the standard is self-generated, or when the report and the standard read the same content, agreement is also what a common cause would produce. Tal's remedy is a second instrument on an independent principle, as when a mercury thermometer is checked against one that does not assume the law of thermal expansion ([SEP §8.2](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/measurement-science/)). [Michel (2021)](https://philpapers.org/archive/MICCIC-2.pdf) sets the same aim for reports: calibration evaluates how reliable a detection procedure is and corrects it. Reliability against a standard that shares the report's inputs is not yet reliability about the internal state.

## Nearest stage

[Comparability](/measuring-self-reports/measurement-card/comparability/) (stage 7). Calibration relates indications to a standard whose value is taken as known. Comparability relates the results of two instruments, neither taken as the standard, and asks whether they agree. A paper that validates self-report against a probe is coded here if it treats the probe as ground truth, and at stage 7 if it treats the probe as a second measurement.
