A study site on measurement in AI introspection research, by Elliot Tower. The paper pages describe what each paper reports at each stage of the [measurement card](/measuring-introspection/measurement-card/), and the [theoretical foundations](/measuring-introspection/foundations/) pages set out the fields the card draws on. Every source is linked where it is cited.
