The eight stages of the [measurement card](/measuring-introspection/measurement-card/) come from seven bodies of work. Metrology supplies the vocabulary and the order of the stages. The philosophy of measurement explains why each stage is needed. Consciousness science and psychometrics have already applied that vocabulary to reports about inner states in people. The philosophy of introspection fixes what the measurand could be when the reporter is a large language model (LLM).

| Field | What it supplies | Card stages |
|---|---|---|
| [Metrology (VIM and GUM)](/measuring-introspection/foundations/metrology/) | The defined terms of measurement: measurand, procedure, indication, measurement model, calibration, traceability, uncertainty, comparability | All eight |
| [Model-based measurement (Tal)](/measuring-introspection/foundations/tal/) | Indication versus outcome; three kinds of calibration; when two instruments measure the same quantity | Instrument and indication, Measurement model, Calibration, Uncertainty, Comparability, Validity conditions |
| [Epistemic iteration (Chang)](/measuring-introspection/foundations/chang/) | Building a standard without a trusted instrument; comparability; carrying a measurement into a new domain | Measurand, Calibration, Comparability, Validity conditions |
| [Robustness and convergence](/measuring-introspection/foundations/robustness/) | Agreement among independent procedures, and how shared methods make it illusory | Calibration, Uncertainty, Comparability |
| [Psychometrics and construct validity](/measuring-introspection/foundations/psychometrics/) | Whether a score measures the attribute it names: construct validity, convergent and discriminant evidence, the causal definition | Measurand, Procedure, Measurement model, Comparability, Validity conditions |
| [Consciousness science (Michel)](/measuring-introspection/foundations/consciousness-science/) | Reports as indications; coordination; two kinds of calibration; criterion problems; validity drift | Measurand, Instrument and indication, Measurement model, Calibration, Uncertainty, Comparability, Validity conditions |
| [Signal detection and metacognition](/measuring-introspection/foundations/signal-detection/) | Sensitivity versus the tendency to say yes; meta-d′ and metacognitive efficiency | Instrument and indication, Measurement model, Calibration, Uncertainty, Comparability, Validity conditions |
| [Philosophy of introspection](/measuring-introspection/foundations/philosophy-of-introspection/) | The senses of introspection a paper can test, from functional self-monitoring to self-specifying access | Measurand, Instrument and indication, Comparability, Validity conditions |

Each page follows the same order: the field and its main ideas, the LLM analog, the limits of the analogy, the card stages it supplies, and linked sources.
